Potential collaborations

Author pairs whose titles are topically similar but who have never co-authored or cited each other. Based on title text only — can be fooled by two people reviewing the same book, or contributors to the same multi-volume series. Treat as leads to check manually, not confirmed overlaps.

About

Nodes are publications matching a Syriac Studies search term set (data: OpenAlex). "Similarity & clusters" combines citation links, shared-reference (bibliographic coupling) and co-citation signals, and title-text similarity into one weighted graph, clustered with the Leiden algorithm. Node size reflects OpenAlex-wide citation count. Early prototype — most cross-references (books, non-English works) are still missing.
